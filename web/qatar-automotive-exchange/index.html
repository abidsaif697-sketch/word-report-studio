Sequence · Hammer to handover

What happens after the hammer

Four steps, in this order, every time. Median elapsed time from hammer to release is 31 hours.

STEP 01

Hammer & lock

Winning bid locks at close. The reserve is disclosed to both parties and the lot leaves the floor.

STEP 02

Escrow funding

Buyer funds a QCB-regulated escrow account. Nothing moves until the balance clears.

STEP 03

Title transfer

Ownership is filed with the Ministry of Interior traffic department and the plate is reassigned.

STEP 04

Release & delivery

Keys release from the Doha compound. Escrow pays the seller on confirmed handover.
