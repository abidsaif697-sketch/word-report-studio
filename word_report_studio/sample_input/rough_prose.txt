Sales Update

This quarter went well overall and the team is happy with the direction.

Riyadh sold 12.4M in Q1 and 15.1M in Q2, while Jeddah recorded 8.2M and 9.6M, and Dammam reached 5.1M and 6.3M.

Revenue grew 18% overall. Customer satisfaction reached 91%. Complaints dropped 22% this quarter.

Our onboarding works in stages. First we run a discovery workshop with the client. Then we design the rollout plan. Next we configure and test the platform. Finally we hand over with training.

The company story so far: in 2022 we started with three people, in 2023 we launched the product publicly, in 2024 we passed 100 clients, and in 2025 we opened the Jeddah office.

Note: renewal season lands in Q3 so account managers need to start early.
