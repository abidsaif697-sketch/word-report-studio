Falcon Edge Consulting company profile draft

Falcon Edge Consulting is a Riyadh based management and technology consulting firm founded in 2015. We help government entities and private companies modernize their operations and digital services.

We are a team of 85 consultants across offices in Riyadh, Dubai and Cairo. Our people combine deep regional knowledge with international delivery standards.

What we do: digital transformation programs, IT strategy and governance, cybersecurity advisory, data analytics platforms, and PMO services for large programs.

The consulting market in the Gulf is growing fast driven by national visions and government digital programs. Demand for cybersecurity and data services is especially strong.

The firm is fully owned by its three founding partners who remain active in client delivery.

CEO message: Since 2015 we have grown from a three person team to a trusted advisor for more than 60 organizations. Our promise is simple, we deliver measurable outcomes not just reports. I am proud of our team and excited about the road ahead. Khalid Al Rashid, Managing Director.

Track record: 60 plus clients served, 240 projects delivered, 96 percent client retention rate, average project rating 4.7 out of 5.
